# Sixing Hu · 胡思行

**Postdoctoral Fellow in Applied Economics**  
School of Management and Economics, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen

I study how digital platforms design information, incentives, and services. My research combines field experiments, causal inference, and analytical models to examine service quality, pricing, gig labor supply, and vehicle relocation.

[University profile](https://sme.cuhk.edu.cn/en/teacher/3417) · [Email](mailto:husixing@cuhk.edu.cn)

## Research interests

- **Platform operations** — service pricing, participation, and resource allocation.
- **Service quality and information design** — quality certification and passenger feedback.
- **Empirical operations management** — field experiments, labor supply, and passenger behavior.

## Working papers

### Revisiting Gig Labor Supply Curves
*Evidence from a Logistics Platform*  
With Yifan Feng and Jussi Keppo · **Under review at MSOM**

Quasi-experimental evidence on how compensation affects worker participation and deliveries per active worker. [Read on SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5168285)

### Vehicle Relocation with Location-Based Compensation
With Bomin Bian, Zhenyu Hu, and Long He · **Under review at MSOM**

An equilibrium model of location-based relocation incentives under uncertain demand and driver competition.

### How Selective Should a Quality Certification Be?
*Evidence from a Ride-Hailing Field Experiment*  
With Yunan Ji, Qiuping Yu, and Yiming Zhang · **Complete draft**

A large-scale field experiment examining how certification coverage affects cancellations and passenger evaluations.

### Balancing Positive and Negative Feedback in Quality Certification
With Yunan Ji, Qiuping Yu, and Yiming Zhang · **Complete draft**

Research on how positive and negative passenger feedback shape certification eligibility.

### Pricing a Vertically Tiered Menu
*Which Price Moves the Market? Evidence from a Ride-Hailing Field Experiment*  
With Yunan Ji, Qiuping Yu, and Yiming Zhang · **Complete draft**

A field experiment examining how standard-tier and premium-tier discounts affect market participation and demand across service tiers.

## Current research

**Driver Service Quality and Customer Lifetime Value**  
Academic–industry research collaboration with DiDi · April 2026–present

I study the relationship between driver service quality and subsequent passenger spending using high-dimensional driver–passenger fixed-effects models, with validation through pre-ride spending placebos, cross-period stability, and out-of-sample tests.
